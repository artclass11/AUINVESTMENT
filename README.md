AUINVEST1 helps people in Nashik and nearby areas enquire about gold and learn how to compare purity, current quotes, purchase documentation and resale terms.

## Website

- Mobile-first landing page in a minimal, premium dark visual system.
- Palette: near-black pine/black, warm ivory/white, restrained muted champagne gold.
- Marathi-first copy with clear English social/profile references.
- WhatsApp enquiry flow (the visitor chooses to open WhatsApp; this site does not store form data).
- Daily rotating gold-literacy tip.
- Live Wix Blog feed with Marathi educational articles.
- Hallmark/HUID guidance, buyer checklist, cost-comparison FAQs and privacy note.
- No hardcoded “live” gold price, guaranteed-return promise, or investment-return claim.

## Sources and trust

Use primary sources such as the Bureau of Indian Standards for hallmarking and HUID guidance. Confirm any purchase quote with its date/time, purity, weight, fees/taxes and buy-back terms before acting. Gold prices can fall as well as rise; no returns are guaranteed.

- BIS hallmarking overview: https://www.bis.gov.in/hallmarking-overview/
- BIS consumer protection: https://www.bis.gov.in/hallmarking-overview/consumer-protection/
