AUINVEST1 helps people in Nashik and nearby areas enquire about gold and learn how to compare purity, current quotes, purchase documentation and resale terms.

## Website
- Mobile-first landing page with a minimal modern editorial design.
- **No gold colours in the brand palette.** Use graphite/near-black, soft white, cool slate and pale silver only.
- Marathi-first copy with clear English social/profile references.
- WhatsApp enquiry flow; visitors choose whether to send a prefilled message.
- Daily rotating gold-literacy tip and Wix Blog feed with Marathi educational articles.
- Hallmark/HUID guidance, buyer checklist, cost-comparison FAQs and privacy note.
- No hardcoded live gold price, guaranteed-return promise, or investment-return claim.

## Permanent visual palette
- Near-black: `#080A0E`
- Graphite: `#10141B`, `#141A22`
- Soft white: `#F1F3F5`
- Muted neutral grey: `#A0A7B0`
- Cool slate accent: `#8794A3`
- Pale silver accent: `#D7DDE4`

**Forbidden for future AUINVEST1 visuals:** gold, champagne, bronze, brass, yellow-gold, warm metallic gradients, gold glows, gold borders or gold-highlighted typography. Gold may be discussed as the product in text, but it must not define the brand's visual palette. The logo remains omitted from post visuals unless the brief changes.

See [VISUAL_STYLE.md](VISUAL_STYLE.md) as the source of truth, and [CONTENT_STRATEGY.md](CONTENT_STRATEGY.md) for content rotation.

## Sources and trust
Use primary sources such as the Bureau of Indian Standards for hallmarking and HUID guidance. Confirm any purchase quote with date/time, purity, weight, fees/taxes and buy-back terms before acting. Gold prices can fall as well as rise; no returns are guaranteed.
- BIS hallmarking overview: https://www.bis.gov.in/hallmarking-overview/
- BIS consumer protection: https://www.bis.gov.in/hallmarking-overview/consumer-protection/
