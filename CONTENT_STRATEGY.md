# AUINVEST1 Daily Content Strategy

## Editorial identity
- Audience: gold buyers and interested investors in Nashik and nearby Maharashtra areas.
- Language: Marathi-first; plain, confident and fact-focused.
- Visual palette: #050706 near-black pine, #0B0F0D panel, #F2F0E8 warm ivory, #BCA16B muted champagne, #D5C18F champagne highlight.
- Minimal modern layout: negative space, few words per card, thin rules, no logo on post visuals, no heavy gradients or clutter.
- CTA: invite people to ask for the *current applicable quote* on WhatsApp +91 9921894495.
- Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/auinvest1/

## Topic rotation
1. 22K vs 24K: explain purity/fineness and product suitability.
2. Hallmark and HUID: verify applicable jewellery hallmark information in the official BIS CARE app.
3. Quote comparison: compare the same purity and weight; include all taxes, fees and premium.
4. Documentation: what a clear invoice should state.
5. Gold bars/coins vs jewellery: distinguish product type and relevant documentation.
6. Buy-back/resale terms: ask about deductions, conditions and proof of purchase.
7. Common buyer questions: answer one real practical question; no fake testimonials.

Repeat the cycle with a new angle and do not reuse the same hook/visual for consecutive days.

## Publication checks
- Cross-check factual hallmarking claims against BIS / primary sources.
- Include a dated quote only if an actual applicable quote is verified; otherwise write “आजचा लागू भाव WhatsApp वर विचारा”.
- Never promise profits, guarantees, risk-free investment, or certain future prices.
- Distinguish gold purchase/sourcing services from regulated financial advice.
- Every website article should be useful, not a thin keyword page.
- For social content, produce a caption, alt text and a source/fact-check note; verify Marathi glyphs and image exports.
- Do not claim that Instagram publishing is active until @auinvest1 is authorized and a test post receives a successful platform confirmation.
