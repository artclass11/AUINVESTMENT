# AUINVEST1 Visual Style — permanent rule

## Absolute colour restriction
**No gold colours in any future AUINVEST1 creation, including the website, carousel posts, stories, reels, thumbnails, banners, profile graphics or other promotional assets.** Do not use champagne, brass, bronze, yellow-gold, warm metallic tones, golden glows, gold gradients, gold borders or gold-highlighted typography. Gold may be discussed as the product, but the brand visuals must not be gold-coloured.

## Approved palette
- Near-black: `#080A0E`
- Graphite surface: `#10141B`
- Deep charcoal panel: `#141A22`
- Soft white: `#F1F3F5`
- Muted copy: `#A0A7B0`
- Cool slate accent: `#8794A3`
- Pale silver accent: `#D7DDE4`
- Border: `#29313B`

## Creative direction
Minimal modern editorial design; generous negative space; concise useful text; legible Marathi/Devanagari; quiet steel/slate accents only; neutral photographic lighting; understated geometry. Premium quality should come from typography, composition and clarity—not metallic decoration. No logo on post visuals unless the user explicitly changes that instruction.

## Content guidelines
Marathi-first gold buying education for Nashik-area customers. Rotate purity/fineness, hallmark and HUID, quote comparisons, taxes/fees, documentation, product-type differences and resale terms. Use date-stamped current quotes only when actually verified; otherwise invite people to request the current applicable quote on WhatsApp `+91 9921894495`. Never promise profit or guaranteed returns, fabricate social proof, or present a stale price as live.

## QA checklist
- Search source files for old metallic palette values and remove them.
- Check homepage, article, insights and privacy page colours.
- Inspect mobile layout and contrast.
- Preview future carousel/reel assets to ensure no gold-coloured accents, gradients or glows.
- Keep this file as the source of truth for every future AUINVEST1 visual creation.
